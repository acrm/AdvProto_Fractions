# Locations

## Nor's Gate
The boom city around the only open descent. Every faction keeps offices, warehouses, shrines, or barracks here. It is the political and logistical heart of the campaign.

## The Gate Market
A quarter of auction houses, debt ledgers, teamsters, black tents, and cargo cages. Economic leverage and resource competition often start here.

## Pilgrim Wells
A shrine-ringed water complex where faith, healing, extortion, and rumor overlap. Religious influence and covert pressure converge here.

## Saltglass Veins
A glittering under-country mineral field. Control of this zone means wealth, alchemical leverage, and violent competition.

## The Switchback Frontier
A network of old fort-tracks and newly cleared descent routes. Whoever secures it shapes military access deeper into the under-country.

## The Hollow Archive
An impossible buried complex of vaults, maps, and machines. It is the key scientific prize and a recurring source of dangerous discoveries.

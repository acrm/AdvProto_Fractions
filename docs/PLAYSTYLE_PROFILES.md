# Playstyle Profiles

## Scope
This document defines playstyle archetypes for player-facing strategy interpretation.
It does not introduce a separate game mode or difficulty system.

## Purpose
Playstyle profiles ensure that roleplay-aligned decisions are treated as valid strategy, even when they diverge from seasonal doctrine recommendations.

## Core Rule
- Seasonal doctrine is advisory.
- Player intent is authoritative.
- Divergence creates friction and opportunity cost, not invalid turns.

## Baseline Profiles

### Opportunist
Primary behavior:
- Frequent pivots when short-term openings appear.
- Willing to trade long-term stability for tactical spikes.

Typical vector bias:
- Positive covertTempo and territorialPressure swings.
- Flexible diplomaticMomentum.

Expected tradeoffs:
- Higher exposure volatility.
- Better comeback potential.

### Stabilizer
Primary behavior:
- Follows doctrine unless contradiction confidence is high.
- Prioritizes low-variance growth and survivability.

Typical vector bias:
- Moderate economicThroughput and diplomaticMomentum.
- Controlled deterrencePosture.

Expected tradeoffs:
- Lower tactical burst.
- Strong consistency in long campaigns.

### Mediator
Primary behavior:
- Invests in diplomatic balance and coalition shaping.
- Uses disruption selectively and defensively.

Typical vector bias:
- High diplomaticMomentum.
- Moderate deterrencePosture.
- Lower covertTempo spikes.

Expected tradeoffs:
- Slower direct objective conversion.
- Better protection from coalition retaliation.

### Saboteur
Primary behavior:
- Prioritizes disruption and denial of rival objectives.
- Accepts increased friction as part of strategy identity.

Typical vector bias:
- High covertTempo and deterrencePosture.
- Lower diplomaticMomentum.

Expected tradeoffs:
- Higher exposure pressure.
- Strong denial capability in contested and mutually-exclusive conflicts.

## Profile and Doctrine Interaction
- Doctrine recommendations should include profile-sensitive interpretation notes.
- High-confidence contradictory intel should reduce profile-agnostic pivot friction.
- Profile selection should influence recommendation interpretation, not objective generation.

## Telemetry Alignment
Track per profile:
- Doctrine adherence ratio.
- Average divergence magnitude.
- Intel-driven pivot frequency.
- Win rate and collapse rate.

Design target:
- No profile should become non-viable across large seed batches.
